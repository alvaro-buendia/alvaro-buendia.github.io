I am a computational and theoretical physicist, focused on the study of the optical response of metallic, dielectric and molecular nanostructures. 

I studied my physics bachelor at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid (UAM), and a master of Quantum Science and Technologies at Universidad del País Vasco/Euskal Herria Unibersitatea (UPV/EHU) in Bilbao. After my master, I came back to my alma mater to work with Elsa Prada on the topological edge states in organic polymers. I started my PhD in October 2020 at the Instituto de Estructura de la Materia from Spanish Research Council (IEM-CSIC), supervised by Dr. Vincenzo Giannini and Prof. José Antonio Sánchez Gil. During my PhD I realized a stay in Prof. Will Barnes' group at University of Exeter, UK. I deffended my thesis "Topological Nanophotonics: Symmetry-protected states of arrays of plasmonic nanoparticles" at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid in December 2024, obtaining Cum Laude and International mentions. 

Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at International Iberian Nanotechnology Laboratory, at Rossier's Group under the supervision of Prof. Nuno M. R. Peres, funded by PATHFINDER project ADAPTATION.
