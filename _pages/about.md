I am a theoretical and computational physicist, with expertise on the study of the optical response of metallic, dielectric and molecular nanostructures.  Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at International Iberian Nanotechnology Laboratory (INL), at [Rossier's Group](https://inl.int/rossier-research-group/) under the supervision of [Prof. Nuno M. R. Peres](https://cf-um-up.pt/index.php/people/12-effective-members/47-nuno-miguel-machado-reis-peres), funded by PATHFINDER project [ADAPTATION](https://adaptation-project.eu/). My research on this project focuses on the analytical and computational study of the optical properties of nanoparticles and microparticles coated by excitonic molecular layers.

I studied a Physics bachelor at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid (UAM), and a master of Quantum Science and Technologies at Universidad del País Vasco/Euskal Herria Unibersitatea (UPV/EHU) in Bilbao.

After my master studies, I came back to my alma mater to work on the group of [Elsa Prada](https://elsaprada.github.io/) on the topic of topological edge states in organic polymers.

I started my PhD in October 2020 at the Instituto de Estructura de la Materia from Spanish Research Council (IEM-CSIC) in the [Optical Spectroscopies on Plasmonic and Semiconductor Nanostructures Group](https://www.iem.cfmac.csic.es/evpm/group_ssasp.html). My thesis was supervised by [Prof. José Antonio Sánchez-Gil](https://www.iem.csic.es/evpm/jsanchez.html) and [Dr. Vincenzo Giannini](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-8025-4964) and focused on the study of topological effects on arrays of plasmonic nanoparticles, such as topological edge states or bound states in the continuum. I deffended [my thesis](https://repositorio.uam.es/entities/publication/3ae98292-3f49-4db9-8559-65c98ac6d071) at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid in December 2024, obtaining Cum Laude and International mentions.
